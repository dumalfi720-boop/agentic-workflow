MODULE 3.4

💰 ROI and pricing

This module teaches you how to argue economic value and defend price based on results, not just hours worked.

6
Topics
45
Minutes
Biz
Level
ROI
Type
1

⏱️ Time saved

Time saved remains the simplest way to calculate returns in many processes.

Main concept

Measure by frequency, volume and baseline before transforming into financial value.

Practical tip

Use conservative estimation. Credibility is worth more than inflated numbers.

2

📉 Reduced error

Smaller error means less rework, less delay and more operational predictability.

Translate into

Hours saved, cost avoided, better SLA or reduced loss.

Common mistake

Treating quality improvement as something “too intangible” to be sold.

3

🛡️ Cost avoided

Many proposals are strong when they show what the company no longer loses.

look at

Delay, loss of opportunity, fine, rework and operational leakage.

Don't do

Using huge figures without a baseline or clear explanation.

4

📈 Revenue generated

In sales and marketing, return may appear as an increase in conversion, coverage or speed.

Main concept

Incremental revenue requires a clear premise and comparison with a baseline.

Alert

Don’t attribute all revenue to automation without considering other factors.

5

🏷️ Price by value

Pricing by value means anchoring it to the result delivered and the delimited scope.

Good anchor

Compare price with estimated gain, mitigated risk and return time.

Weak anchor

Explain value only by number of hours or technical complexity.

6

🧪 Calculation laboratory

Closing with a lean ROI calculator and pricing range.

Exercise

Create a spreadsheet or calculation block with baseline, expected earnings and suggested price.

Deliverable

Simple ROI calculator with pricing argument.

✅ Final summary

✓ Learned to estimate returns carefully.

✓ Translates time, error and cost into value.

✓ Understood incremental revenue limits.

✓ Started anchoring price to results.

✓ Left with a defendable commercial calculation.
