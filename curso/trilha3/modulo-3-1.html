MODULE 3.1

🔎 Diagnosis of real bottlenecks

This module teaches you how to move beyond enthusiasm with technology and into concrete opportunities: process, waste, eligibility and priority.

6
Topics
45
Minutes
Biz
Level
Discovery
Type
1

🗺️ Map process

The first job is to understand how the process works today, not to imagine how you would like it to be.

Main concept

Mapping creates clarity about inputs, outputs, actors and systems involved.

Practical tip

Design the current flow in simple steps before talking about tools or agents.

2

🕳️ Find waste

Rework, delays, queues, errors and low standardization are signs of real opportunity.

Observe

Waiting, repetition, human error, bad handoff and low traceability.

Don't confuse

Visible symptom with root cause of the bottleneck.

3

🎯 Choose eligible problem

Not every problem deserves an agentic project. Eligibility requires value, data, repetition and viability.

Technical-commercial criteria

Look for frequent, measurable problems with the possibility of clear gains in the short term.

Common mistake

Choose a case that is beautiful to present, but rare or without access to data.

4

⛔ When not to use agentic

Commercial maturity includes saying no when another simple solution works better.

Main concept

Fixed automation, process change or better training may trump the agentic option.

Alert

Pushing agents in an inappropriate context burns the relationship and the case.

5

⚠️ Risk and priority

After finding opportunities, I need to sort out what comes first and what should wait.

Prioritize

Impact, volume, ease of implementation and chance of adoption.

Mitigate

Data risk, external dependence and process criticality.

6

🧪 Discovery laboratory

Closing with an opportunity map that already serves as the basis for a proposal or commercial backlog.

Exercise

Choose a real process, map bottlenecks, rank opportunities and justify the initial case.

Deliverable

Opportunity map with priority, risk and eligible problem.

✅ Final summary

✓ Mapped the process before proposing a solution.

✓ Identified waste and real opportunity.

✓ Filtered problems eligible for agentic.

✓ Understood when not to use the approach.

✓ Came out with a commercially useful discovery.
