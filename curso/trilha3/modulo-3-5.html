MODULE 3.5

🚚 Delivery and adoption

This module focuses on after the sale: onboarding, access, acceptance, rollout and support model.

6
Topics
45
Minutes
Biz
Level
Rollout
Type
1

👋 Onboarding

Onboarding defines the pace and quality of implementation much more than most people imagine.

Main concept

A good kickoff aligns roles, goals, access and expectations of acceptance.

Practical tip

Use a fixed onboarding checklist to avoid relying on memory.

2

🔑 Access and credentials

Operational delays usually arise here, not in the code.

To guarantee

Account, owner, environment, secret and approval path.

Risk

Start running without critical accesses mapped.

3

✅ Validation of acceptance

Accept takes delivery out of the subjective field and defines what “ready” means.

Set

Guided test, objective criteria and validation evidence.

Common mistake

Depend on what “looks good” from the client without a formal framework.

4

🚀 Rollout

Good implementation tends to be gradual, with pilot and controlled expansion.

Main concept

Rollout per phase reduces risk, improves learning and protects the case.

Alert

Big bang in a sensitive process without a pilot increases the chance of rejection.

5

🛠️ Maintenance and SLA

After the rollout, the customer needs to know how support and evolution work.

Cover

Response time, priority, window and improvement criteria.

Don't do

Promise broad support without a defined model.

6

🧪 Handoff laboratory

Closing with an implementation plan and initial support.

Exercise

Create an onboarding, acceptance, rollout and maintenance plan.

Deliverable

Rollout plan with handoff and support model.

✅ Final summary

✓ Structured onboarding and access.

✓ Defined acceptance and evidence.

✓ Understood gradual rollout.

✓ Organized maintenance and SLA.

✓ Left with a clear handoff plan.
