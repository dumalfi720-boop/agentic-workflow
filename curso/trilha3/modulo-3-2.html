MODULE 3.2

🧭 Use cases by area

This module creates a repertoire applicable by business area, without falling into generic or irrelevant examples for real sales.

6
Topics
45
Minutes
Biz
Level
Use Cases
Type
1

📣 Marketing

Marketing offers clear cases in research, briefing, content and performance analysis.

Main concept

The area combines high volume, low standardization and the need for speed.

Practical tip

Avoid selling “automatic creation”; focus on research flow, briefing and operational support.

2

💼 Sales

Prospecting, qualification and commercial preparation are uses with returns that are generally easy to demonstrate.

Apply to

Enrichment, lead scoring, follow-up and meeting preparation.

Avoid

Promise of total automation of the commercial relationship without supervision.

3

🎧 Service

Service teaches well the value of screening, human approval and correct handoff.

Operational criteria

Classifying, prioritizing and suggesting a response is usually safer than answering everything yourself.

Common fault

Confusing a broad chatbot with a truly controlled service flow.

4

🏭 Operations

Backoffice, reconciliation and manual processing often hide relevant financial gains.

Main concept

The best case is not always the most visible. Operations have a lot of silent value.

Alert

Do not oversimplify processes that depend on exceptions and traceability.

5

💳 Finance and HR

These areas require great care with sensitive data, approval and access policy.

Good use

Screening, analytical support, document organization and assisted flow.

High risk

Irreversible decision without supervision in a regulated context.

6

🧪 Sector laboratory

Closing a backlog of cases by area, priority and level of risk.

Exercise

Select a company or sector and list 10 cases organized by area and impact.

Deliverable

Sectoral backlog with prioritized cases and risk observations.

✅ Final summary

✓ Created a repertoire by business area.

✓ Understood differences between functions and risks.

✓ Organized use cases by priority.

✓ Gained useful material for discovery and sale.

✓ Left with a reusable sector backlog.
