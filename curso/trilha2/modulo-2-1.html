MODULE 2.1

🏗️ Agentic systems architecture

This module organizes runtime, components, steps, states and boundaries between deterministic automation and agentic decision.

6
Topics
45

core

Arch

1

🧱 Runtime and components

Architecture begins by understanding who executes, who decides, who persists and who observes.

Main concept

Mature agentic system separates runtime, orchestration, agent, tools and storage.

Practical tip

Draw the components before writing the flow. This avoids hidden coupling.

2

🪜 Execution steps

Each execution needs an observable beginning, middle and end. This allows you to locally validate each step.

To do

Define entry, processing, validation and delivery by phase.

Avoid

Single and diffuse flow without knowing where each decision takes place.

3

🔀 Decision points

Agent autonomy needs to enter at specific and justifiable points, not just anywhere in the system.

Technical criteria

Use agentic decision when there is controlled ambiguity and real value of contextual judgment.

Common mistake

Give autonomy to steps that should be fixed, cheap and predictable.

4

🗄️ Queues, states and persistence

Queues and storage support resumption, asynchrony and scalability without loss of operational information.

Main concept

If the flow depends on time, event or reprocessing, it needs persistent state and a clear checkpoint.

Alert

Without organized persistence, each failure becomes a complete restart or loss of traceability.

5

⚖️ Borders between deterministic and agentic

This is the central design of the trail. The right boundary defines cost, risk, latency and ease of debugging.

Deterministic

Persistence, validation, triggers and sensitive side effects.

Agentico

Classification, strategy selection, prioritization and local planning.

6

🧪 Architecture laboratory

Closing the module with a complete operational diagram of a real case, including runtime, phases, state and decision points.

Exercise

Design a workflow with components, state, inputs, outputs and failure routes.

Deliverable

Operational diagram with deterministic and agentic boundaries.

✅ Final summary

✓ Organized runtime, components and phases.

✓ Understood where autonomy should come in.

✓ Saw the role of state, queue and persistence.

✓ Separated fixed flow from agentic judgment.

✓ You came out with an operational diagram of the system.
