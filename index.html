Target training

Talents that the course aims to develop

Define operational objectives with clear success and failure criteria.
Design tools with schema, scope and human approval when necessary.
Correctly separate deterministic flow from agentic steps.
Create evals, read traces and debug regression without breaking what already works.
Deal with security, injection, unreliable data and destructive operations.
Operate workflows in production with logs, costs and observability.
